Our Heritage & Craft

Rooted in Passion, Brewed with Conscience

Founded in 2014 by lifelong coffee enthusiasts, Aroma Roasters began with a restored vintage 5kg drum roaster and an unwavering mission: make extraordinary specialty coffee accessible, unpretentious, and environmentally thoughtful.

Every bean that enters our roastery is hand-selected through direct partnerships with independent family farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, Guatemala, and Sumatra. We roast on custom-tailored thermodynamic profiles that respect the natural altitude, soil, and terroir of each harvest.

Direct-Trade Ethos

We pay 40% above fair-trade minimums directly to farming cooperatives.

Precision Profiling

Small micro-batches roasted every morning for peak aromatic freshness.

Community Haven

Comfortable seating, acoustic warmth, and complimentary gigabit Wi-Fi.

Zero-Waste Goal

Spent grounds are donated to community soil gardens; fully recycled packaging.
